# Personas

Five people have a stake: two free users and buyers, two professionals, and the operator. All are fictional and need validating in interviews.

| Persona | Who | Wants | Frustration today | Tier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Jonas, the dial-in hobbyist** | 34, engineer, espresso machine and hand grinder at home | Remember which grind, dose and ratio worked for which bag | Notes app entries he never finds again; forgets the recipe by the next bag | Free |
| **Mira, the explorer** | 28, buys a new bag every two weeks, filter and AeroPress | Know before paying 15 to 20 EUR whether a bean suits her taste and method | Review sites are prose opinions she cannot compare; star ratings say nothing about brew method | Paid |
| **Tomás, the café owner** | 41, runs a café with rotating guest roasters | A fast starting recipe for a new bean; evidence for which beans to stock | Burns 200 to 300 g dialling in each new bean | Paid |
| **Selin, the micro-roaster** | 38, roasts 40 kg a week, sells online | See how customers actually brew and rate her beans; correct catalogue data | Feedback arrives as Instagram comments, no structure | Paid; later a roaster tier |
| **The operator** | Solo developer and owner | Near-zero fixed cost, no moderation workload, clean data, GDPR-safe | A review app with free text needs moderators, spam filters and translation | n/a |

What the personas agree on: structured input is faster than typing, and comparable data is worth more than opinions.

Where they conflict: Jonas wants depth in the brew log, Mira wants a 20-second flow. The spec resolves this with saved recipes and optional detail.
